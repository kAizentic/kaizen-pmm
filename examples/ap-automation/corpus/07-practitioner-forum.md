# Thread: "Is anyone's AP module actually handling approvals?"

*Paraphrased from a fictional practitioner forum. Usernames removed.*

**Original post (AP lead, distribution company, ~3,000 invoices a month):** We turned on our ERP's AP
module last year. Capture is great. Approvals are still a nightmare: department heads ignore the
notifications, and I spend Mondays chasing people on chat. Is anyone's setup actually working for
approvals?

**Reply 1 (controller, nonprofit):** Same. The module routes to one approver and stops. No
escalation, no delegation when someone is on leave. We built reminder emails in a spreadsheet macro.

**Reply 2 (AP specialist, retail):** Exceptions are worse than approvals for us. Every PO mismatch
turns into a 10-email thread. When an auditor asks about one, I search my inbox.

**Reply 3 (AP manager, logistics):** We looked at outsourcing. Their per-invoice price was good, but
exceptions still came back to us, so the savings mostly disappeared.

**Reply 4 (controller, software):** We tried a standalone AP suite that replaced the ERP's payables
entirely. The migration took eight months and IT still talks about it. I would never do a rip-and-
replace for AP again.

**Reply 5 (original poster):** Thanks, all. Sounds like nobody has solved approvals inside the ERP.
