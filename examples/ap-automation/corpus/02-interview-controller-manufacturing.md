# Interview: corporate controller, industrial manufacturer

**Company:** 620 employees, three plants, about 4,200 supplier invoices a month.
**Role:** corporate controller; accounts payable (four people) reports to them.

## Notes

The team adopted the Contoso Finance Suite AP module in 2024. Capture works: "the invoices get into
the system fine now. They just sit there." The controller described plant managers as the
bottleneck. "A plant manager has forty invoices waiting and approves them on a Friday afternoon,
in a batch, without looking."

Month-end close slipped from day 6 to day 9 over the last year. The controller blames late
approvals: accruals cannot be finalized while invoices are still waiting on an approver.

Exceptions are the second pain. "When a receipt doesn't match the PO we email the plant, the plant
emails the supplier, and three weeks later someone remembers." Last year's audit found a duplicate
payment of $38,000 that had gone through as a resolved exception. Nobody could say who approved the
override.

## On alternatives

- Asked Contoso about routing rules: the module supports one approver per cost center and no
  escalation. "It's a workflow for a company with one approver."
- Rejected outsourcing: "I'm not handing my exceptions to someone who doesn't know our plants."
- Would evaluate a dedicated approval layer if it did not mean replacing the ERP. IT has no
  capacity for a migration before the next fiscal year.

## Quotes

- "I don't need faster capture. I need the approvals to happen."
- "If an auditor asks why we paid an invoice, I want to click on it and see who decided and why."
