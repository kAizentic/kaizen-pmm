# Interview: CFO, B2B software company

**Company:** 410 employees, Series C, about 1,800 supplier invoices a month, preparing for an audit
by a larger firm ahead of a possible IPO.
**Role:** CFO; the controller and a two-person AP team report to them.

## Notes

The CFO frames accounts payable as a controls problem rather than a cost problem. "Our volume is low.
What I can't have is a material weakness in payment controls the year we file."

The board's audit committee asked in Q2 for evidence of segregation of duties on payments. Today
approvals happen in email and Slack. Reconstructing who approved a given payment takes the
controller about 40 minutes per invoice sampled, and the auditors sampled 60.

The company also had a vendor bank-change fraud attempt in 2025: a spoofed supplier email requested
new payment details. It was stopped only because the amount was unusually large and the controller
asked a question.

## On alternatives

- Already licenses the Contoso Finance Suite, and the AP module was "the default answer." The CFO's
  concern is that approval policies cannot vary by amount and department.
- Budget: would fund a tool if it removed the need to hire a third AP specialist, estimated at
  $95,000 fully loaded.
- Timeline: wants controls in place before the audit fieldwork starts in Q1 2027.

## Quotes

- "Show me the approval trail and the bank-change verification, and I'll sign this quarter."
- "I'm not buying automation. I'm buying evidence."
