# Interview: accounts payable manager, multi-site clinic group

**Company:** 1,100 employees across 23 clinics, about 9,000 supplier invoices a month.
**Role:** accounts payable manager with a team of six.

## Notes

The group outsourced accounts payable to Fabrikam Payables Services in 2023 and brought it back in
house in early 2026. The reason was exceptions. "They processed the clean invoices beautifully.
Everything that didn't match came back to us anyway, except now it came back late and without
context."

Exceptions are about one invoice in five, mostly medical supply orders where the quantity received
differs from the purchase order. Each exception needs a clinic manager to confirm what arrived.
Clinic managers answer email slowly; the team keeps a spreadsheet of open exceptions that runs to
roughly 300 rows at any time.

Staff turnover is the hidden cost. Two of six AP specialists left in the last year. "Every exception
lives in someone's inbox. When they leave, the history leaves with them."

## Fraud

The team caught a fake bank-detail change request in March: an email that looked like it came from a
real supplier asked for payments to go to a new account. It was caught because a specialist happened
to call the supplier. There is no formal verification step.

## On alternatives

- Outsourcing: "Cheaper per invoice on paper. It cost us more in exceptions."
- ERP module: the group's ERP has an AP module but it does not route to clinic managers who are not
  ERP users.
- Wants approvals that clinic managers can complete without an ERP login, and a record of every
  exception decision.
